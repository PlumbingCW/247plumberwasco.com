Each one is written for homes in Wasco and nearby Shafter and McFarland.

How to use these guides

Read the guide that matches your symptom, try only the steps marked safe, and stop where it tells you to stop. A guide cannot see inside your wall, so none of them replaces a plumber looking at the actual pipe.

If water is already spreading or you smell gas, skip the reading. Leave a gas smell first and call SoCalGas or 911 from outside, then reach the 24/7 line. For anything else the guides can wait a few minutes while you close the main valve.

Where the facts in these guides come from

Local points are drawn from published city reports, city codes and state agencies rather than from guesswork. Where a fact could not be confirmed, the guide says to check with the City of Wasco or read your city's water quality report.

We do not print dollar figures for repairs. Cost guides explain what moves a quote so you can compare estimates, and the price you pay is set by the plumber before work begins.

When a guide turns into a service call

The same rule runs through every article: California requires a contractor license for work above a state-set threshold, and plumbing that touches gas, sewer or water heaters generally belongs with a licensed plumber. You can check any contractor at the CSLB website before they begin.
