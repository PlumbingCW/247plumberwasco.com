What we know about Palm Avenue, and what we do not

Public property listings show addresses on Palm Avenue from number 141 up to 16409. A street that long runs from built-up blocks out toward farm parcels, so two Palm Avenue addresses can be very different places.

Palm Avenue Middle School, in the Wasco Union Elementary School District, shares the name. Oyster List describes the neighborhood as family oriented with quiet streets. We treat that as a description, not a measured fact, and no source we found draws a boundary.

Because of that, we use Palm Avenue as an anchor street. Tell us your house number and nearest cross street, and we will say whether this page fits. Not every address on the road is necessarily inside the city limits, which matters for the city rules below. Homes near 7th Street have a separate page, Downtown Wasco.

Leaks and watering under Wasco's water code

Wasco's water conservation chapter sets staged restrictions. On staged days, watering is allowed only from midnight to 5 a.m. and from 7 p.m. to 11:59 p.m. The city sets the current stage, so check with the city before you assume a schedule.

According to the same chapter, leaks must be fixed, and it treats 48 hours after discovery as a reasonable time to do it. A dripping hose bib or a wet patch in the yard is not a problem to leave for a month.

These rules apply to city water customers. If you are not sure whether your address is on city water, the water bill will tell you.

Replacing a water heater: the permit question we cannot answer for you

We could not find a Wasco rule on whether a water heater replacement needs a city permit, and the city's permit portal would not open for us. Do not rely on anyone, including us, to state it from memory.

What we can say is that Wasco's code adopts the 2022 California Plumbing Code by reference, while the state's 2025 edition took effect on January 1, 2026. Ask Community Development at (661) 758-7200 what applies before you book a water heater replacement. State law also requires water heaters to be braced against earthquakes, which a plumber should check on any swap.
