Three McFarland emergencies and the first move for each

Start by naming what you see. Water spraying or pooling means closing the main valve. Drains gurgling or backing up means stopping every tap, toilet and appliance on that line. Reading the city's water report before you ask about filters

McFarland's water comes from city-run groundwater wells. The 2025 report, dated May 1, 2026, lists hardness at 11 ppm, which the numbers show as soft water. Scale from the city supply is therefore less likely, though flushing a tank still keeps sediment from collecting.

The same report describes arsenic treatment at the Garzoli well and an emergency reverse osmosis system for nitrate and 1,2,3-TCP at the Browning Road well, and it lists related notices.
