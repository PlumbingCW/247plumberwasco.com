No hot water or a leaking heater: what to check right now

If the heater is leaking or the house has no hot water, call the 24/7 line and a licensed plumber can be scheduled, with same-day appointments when available. Until then, shut the cold water valve above the tank, turn off the breaker or gas control, and stop using hot taps. Do not touch a heater, cord or breaker while standing in water.

Water heater repair in Wasco, CA is the diagnosis and replacement of failed parts, such as a thermostat, heating element, gas control, igniter, valve or anode rod, so a sound tank keeps heating. The first job is separating a part failure from a tank failure. A cracked element gasket or a dripping valve is a part. Water seeping from the shell is the tank.

Many callers describe the same handful of symptoms: a heater that goes out after lighting, water that is warm but not hot, a pan with water in it, and a stainless tank flashing an error code. Each of those narrows to a short list of causes, which is why a few minutes of testing usually beats guessing.

Pilot, thermostat, element and anode: what each one does

Knowing the part makes the quote easier to read. These four account for many service calls.

  • ✓Pilot and thermocouple On a standing-pilot gas heater, the thermocouple senses the flame and holds the gas valve open. A weak or dirty one lets the pilot drop out within seconds of letting go of the knob.
  • ✓Thermostat It tells the burner or elements when to run. A failed one can leave water cold, or overheat it until the relief valve opens.
  • ✓Heating elements Electric tanks usually have an upper and a lower element. If only the lower fails, you get a short supply of hot water that runs out quickly.
  • ✓Anode rod This sacrificial metal rod corrodes so the tank lining does not. Swapping a worn rod can extend tank life, but once the shell leaks, the rod cannot help.

If you only want the anode checked on an older unit, ask. It is a small job with a clear answer.

Sediment, flushing and what the city report can tell you

Mineral sediment settles at the bottom of a tank, insulates the burner or lower element, and makes the tank rumble as water boils underneath it. A flush clears loose sediment and is routine upkeep on a healthy tank. On an older tank with a thick layer, flushing may help only a little, so the plumber checks first.

City of Wasco water comes from groundwater wells, and mineral levels differ from one city to the next. Wasco's water quality report is the place to look, and if it does not list hardness, a test strip kit tells you more than any guess. Shafter's and McFarland's own reports differ from each other too, so no neighbor's number should drive your decision.

Gas, electric and hybrid heaters: three different repairs

Gas tank. Faults center on ignition, the flame sensor, the gas control valve and the vent. Anything involving gas piping or a gas valve is plumbing-license work, and the C-36 classification covers gas appliance connections.

Electric tank. Faults center on elements, thermostats, high-limit switches and the breaker or wiring that feeds them. The plumber tests parts with a meter before replacing anything, because a reset or one failed element is a smaller job than a swap.

Hybrid heat pump tank. These pair a heat pump with backup elements. Common issues are a blocked air filter, a clogged condensate drain, a fan fault or a unit that has dropped into electric-only mode. The maker's error code points to the cause, and the plumber can run the tank on backup heat while a part is on order.

Gas appliance safety comes first

A gas smell, hissing near the heater, or soot around the burner is not a repair to wait on. Leave the building on foot, then call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 or 911 from outside. SoCalGas serves Wasco, Shafter and McFarland. Do not try to relight a heater you suspect is leaking gas.

Deciding between one more repair and a new heater

The Department of Energy says storage water heaters last 10 to 15 years. A tank at the far end of that range with a leak, rusty water or repeated failures is a replacement candidate, and a younger unit with one dead part is a repair candidate. Heaters that need constant resets or relighting are worth a planned installation while you have time to choose.
