There is one way to reach us and it works at every hour: call the number on this page. The line is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

We do not use contact forms or a chat box, because a form cannot tell a dispatcher that water is spreading toward an outlet.

Phone only, on purpose

A form submitted at 2 a.m. sits unread until someone checks it. A phone call reaches the dispatch line while the problem is still small enough to limit. That is the reasoning behind a call-only site, and it applies to a slow drain as much as to a burst pipe.

When you call, you will talk with the line about what is happening, where you are, and what you can safely do while a licensed plumber is lined up. Planned jobs are welcome too. Say the work is not urgent and ask about same-day appointments when available.

Have these ready before you dial

You can still call without any of this, but a few details save minutes on the line.

  • ✓The street address and a cross street. A cross street helps the dispatcher confirm the right property.
  • ✓What you see, hear or smell. A stain on a ceiling, a hissing sound, a gurgling drain or no hot water each point the dispatcher toward a different kind of plumber.
  • ✓Whether the water is off. If you found and closed the main valve, say so. If you could not find it, say that too, and the dispatcher will help.
  • ✓Access details. Gate codes, a locked garage, a dog in the yard or a tenant who needs notice all change how the visit goes.
  • ✓A callback number that works. Give a number that will be answered, and keep that phone charged and nearby while you wait for the plumber.
  • ✓Your role at the property. Owners, tenants and property managers all call us. Mention which you are, since a tenant may need the owner's approval for larger work.

If it is happening right now

Take these steps while you dial or immediately after the call. They apply before any plumber arrives.

Safe first moves

  • !Water flowing where it should not. Close the main shutoff valve where the line enters the house. Shutting the main also keeps the water already in the water heater and toilet tanks from draining out.
  • !Wet floors near electrical items. Stay away from outlets, power strips and appliances standing in water. Switch power off only if the breaker panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping through water.
  • !Sewage coming up. Keep children and pets out of the area and do not flush or run water. Avoid touching the spill with bare hands.
  • !A smell of gas. Leave the building on foot right away. Do not use switches or phones inside, and call SoCalGas or 911 from outside. Call us after you are safe.

None of these steps replaces a plumber. They buy time.

Before you call

Requests start by phone. A written message can wait unread for hours, and plumbing problems usually get worse in that time. Calling is the fastest way to reach a person.

The line is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Night and weekend calls are routine, and the same questions get asked as at noon on a weekday.

The address is the business address listed in the footer and on the map. Dispatch happens by phone and service happens at your property, so call before traveling anywhere.

Say so at the start of the call and give the unit address plus a tenant contact if there is one. Access is the usual delay with rental units, so having the tenant expect the plumber helps.

The call is for describing the problem and arranging the visit. The price for the work is agreed with the plumber before work starts, so you decide after you hear it.
