What this site and line do

Wasco 24/7 Plumbing runs a website and a 24/7 phone line for plumbing requests in Wasco, Shafter and McFarland, California. The line takes the details of a problem and connects you with a licensed, insured plumber.

The website is informational. You cannot book, pay or submit a request through it, and every request starts with a phone call to the number shown on each page.

Who does the plumbing work

The plumber who comes to your property performs the work and is responsible for it, including the quality of the work and compliance with applicable codes. The dispatch line is responsible for taking your call and arranging the match.

We encourage you to confirm any plumber's license with the Contractors State License Board at cslb.ca.gov before work begins. The about page explains how.

Prices and agreements

The price for a job is set by the plumber after seeing the problem, and it is explained before work starts. Nothing said on the dispatch call or printed on this site is a binding price quote for a specific job.

If the plumber finds a different problem from the one you described, the price may change, and you can approve the new scope or decline it. Any contract for the work is between you and the plumber.

Availability and timing

The line is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Same-day appointments are offered when available, and we do not guarantee a particular arrival time, a visit window or that a plumber can reach every address on every day.

Weather, road conditions, the nature of the problem and demand on a given night all affect scheduling. The dispatcher will tell you honestly what is possible when you call.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. See the privacy policy for how recordings and call details are handled.
