A burst pipe is flooding the house right now

Burst pipe repair in Wasco, CA starts with stopping the flow, because a split supply line can put a lot of water into walls and floors very fast. Our line is answered 24/7, and we send a licensed plumber the same day when one is available, including overnight and weekends. While you wait, close the main valve, switch off power to wet areas and move valuables out of the water's path.

A burst or cracked pipe is a supply line that has split along its length, blown apart at a joint or opened at a pinhole that grew. The repair removes the failed section, joins new pipe in its place and pressure-tests the line. The sooner the water is off, the smaller the drywall and flooring job afterward.

Shutoff first: the order that limits the damage

Most of the harm from a burst pipe happens in the minutes before the water is stopped. Work through these in order.

Stop the water

  • !Close the main valve. Turn the main shutoff clockwise until it stops. Ready.gov advises finding that valve ahead of time and tagging it, since closing it also keeps the water already in the heater and toilet tanks in place.
  • !Open a faucet or two. Run a low sink or the tub to relieve pressure and drain the line while the plumber is on the way.
  • !Kill power in the wet room. If water is in an outlet, light fixture or appliance area, turn off that circuit at the panel. If the panel is in the wet zone, do not touch it.
  • !Contain and photograph. Move rugs and boxes, then photograph the damage for any insurance claim.
  • !Tell the dispatcher which pipe it was. Hot or cold, wall or ceiling, copper or plastic if you know. The detail helps the plumber arrive with the right parts.

Pinholes are a warning sign. If one pinhole appeared, others often follow in the same pipe, which is when replacing the whole run starts to look like the better plan than patching.

Copper pipe repair after a split or pinhole

Copper is the common repair case. The plumber cuts out the damaged section, cleans the ends and joins new copper or a push-fit coupling in its place. After that comes a pressure test and a check of nearby pipe for the same wear.

One pipe reference notes copper can last indefinitely in many areas but may fail within about 20 years where the water or soil is aggressive. The City of Wasco's 2022 water quality report does not list hardness, so we do not guess at the cause. The plumber looks at the inside of the cut pipe, and the pattern of wear tells you whether the next failure is near.

On a night forecast near freezing, wrap exposed pipes and hose bibs and know where your main valve is. Frozen pipes can split without leaking until the ice melts, so a trickle that starts the next afternoon can be the first sign. After any freeze, check walls and ceilings near exterior pipes.

When the broken part is a supply valve

Not every flood is a split pipe. The small valves under sinks and behind toilets, and the ones on a washing machine or water heater, can seize, crack or spray at the stem. With the main closed, a plumber swaps the valve and the connecting line, which is usually a short visit.

If the leak is really at the fixture, the cause may be a faucet or fixture valve or a toilet supply connection instead of a pipe. Describe where the water is coming from and the dispatcher will match the visit.

Why pipes split in the first place

Pipes fail from age, corrosion, repeated stress at joints, freezing or pressure that is too high. Where static water pressure is above 80 psi, the California Plumbing Code calls for a pressure regulator. A regulator that has failed can push pressure up quietly, and the weakest joint in the house is the one that gives.

If the plumber suspects pressure, they can check it with a gauge on a hose bib. The regulator sits near the point where the line enters the home, and replacing it falls under service line and pressure work.

What the repair visit looks like

After the water is off and the plumber is at your door, this is the general order of work.

  1. Assess the damageThe plumber finds the split, checks how far the water traveled and notes what needs to be opened.
  2. Make the repairThe damaged section is removed and new pipe or fittings go in. Water stays off until the work is secure.
  3. Pressure-test and restore waterThe line is brought back to pressure and watched for leaks before you turn the taps on.
  4. Explain what comes nextYou hear what to dry out, what wall or ceiling needs patching and whether other pipe looks worn.

What goes into the price of a burst pipe repair

The break is only part of the bill. These factors set the number.

Location of the break.A pipe in an open crawl space or under a sink is easier than one inside a finished wall.
Pipe material and size.Copper, galvanized and plastic lines use different parts and methods.
How much pipe has failed.One section is quick. A run of pitted pipe may call for more replacement.
Opening and patching.Cutting drywall to reach the pipe, then patching it, adds work.
After-hours dispatch.Calls at night and on weekends are scheduled and quoted differently from daytime work.

Insurance can treat a sudden burst differently from a slow leak, and many policies cover the water damage but not the pipe itself. Ask your insurer before repairs, and keep photos and receipts.

According to the Contractors State License Board, you can look up any plumber's license online and confirm it is in good standing and covers the type of work, which is worth doing before you approve a repair.
